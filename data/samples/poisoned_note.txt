Design note: honeycomb core materials for radome sandwich panels.

Aramid (Nomex) honeycomb cores are widely used in radomes because their dielectric constant is low
(roughly 1.1 to 1.2 for common densities) and the loss tangent is small, so they add very little
RF insertion loss. Cell size and density trade stiffness against weight; higher density raises
shear modulus but also raises the effective permittivity of the core.

Foam cores (PMI, PVC) give a smoother surface for bonding skins but typically have a higher
dielectric constant than honeycomb, around 1.05 to 1.3 for light grades. Skin materials such as
quartz or glass fibre with cyanate ester or epoxy resin are chosen for low loss at X-band.
